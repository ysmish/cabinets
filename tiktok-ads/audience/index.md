# Audience

> **Replace with your own.** The Creative Lead uses this to choose the angle and
> the register. Demographics alone are close to useless here — the useful part is
> what these people find embarrassing, funny, or obvious.

## Who they are

Age, where they live, what their day looks like. Two or three sentences.

*Example: 22–30, urban, renting, mildly cynical about brands that talk about
values.*

## What they already believe

The prior we are writing against. An ad that ignores this reads as naive.

*Example: They assume every reusable bottle brand is interchangeable and that
the sustainability angle is marketing.*

## How they talk

Words they use, words they would never use. Feed this into hooks directly.

## Where the attention is

What else is on their feed. An ad that looks unlike everything around it gets
skipped; an ad that looks exactly like everything around it gets ignored.

## Angles worth testing

A running list. The Growth Analyst updates this from the weekly learning note,
so it should get better over time rather than staying static.

- *Problem-first*
- *Ritual / habit*
- *Contrarian take*
- *Objection handling*
- *Demonstration*
