# Product

> **Replace with your own.** One section per product you advertise.

## <Product name>

**What it is:** one sentence.

**The job it does:** the thing the customer is actually trying to accomplish.
Not the feature — the outcome.

**Features worth showing on camera:** features that are *visual* are worth far
more in a TikTok ad than features that need explaining. List them separately.

| Feature | Visual? | How it looks on screen |
|---|---|---|
| *Leakproof lid* | Yes | *Bottle upside down in a bag, nothing happens* |
| *12-year warranty* | No | *Text overlay only* |

**Price and offer:** what we can say about price, and what we cannot.

**Objections:** what makes people hesitate. Objection-handling is one of the
strongest TikTok ad angles, so be specific.
