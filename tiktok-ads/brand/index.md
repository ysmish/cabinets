# Brand

> **Replace everything below with your own.** The Creative Lead reads this file
> before writing a single hook. Vague input here produces generic ads; the
> "never mention" list does more work than anything else on this page.

## What we sell

One or two sentences. Plain language, no positioning-deck vocabulary.

*Example: We make reusable steel water bottles that don't leak and don't need
replacing.*

## Voice

Three adjectives, then the rules that follow from them.

*Example: Dry, understated, unhurried. Never uses exclamation marks. Never uses
the word "revolutionary". Sentences are short.*

## Never mention

An explicit blocklist. This is the most useful section on the page — it is much
easier for an agent to obey a prohibition than to infer taste.

*Example: competitors by name; discounts or urgency; sustainability guilt;
anything implying the customer is failing at something.*

## Visual identity

Colours, typography, and the look of the world our ads live in. This feeds
directly into Veo prompts, so describe it as you would describe a photograph.

*Example: Cold morning light, matte surfaces, kitchens and desks rather than
mountains. Muted palette — steel, off-white, one warm accent. No stock-footage
gloss.*

## Proof points

Facts an ad may state. Everything here must be true; the Creative Lead is
instructed not to invent claims.

*Example: 12-year warranty. 4,100 five-star reviews. Made in Portugal.*
