# Brand

## What we sell
Reusable steel water bottles that don't leak and don't need replacing.

## Voice
Dry, understated, unhurried. Never uses exclamation marks. Never uses the word
"revolutionary". Sentences are short.

## Never mention
Competitors by name. Discounts or urgency. Sustainability guilt. Anything
implying the customer is failing at something.

## Visual identity
Cold morning light, matte surfaces, kitchens and desks rather than mountains.
Muted palette — steel, off-white, one warm accent. No stock-footage gloss.

## Proof points
12-year warranty. 4,100 five-star reviews. Made in Portugal.
